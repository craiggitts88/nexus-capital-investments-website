Under The Hood

How The Calculation Works

No magic — just three small steps that convert the spread from the units MT5 reports into the units SQX expects.

Step 1
Point Size
From your Digits: 1 / 10^Digits. This is the size of one point for the symbol.
Step 2
Actual Spread
The Market Watch value in real price terms: MarketWatch × Pip/Tick Step.
Step 3
SQX Entry
Convert into SQX's units: Actual Spread / Pip/Tick Size. That result is your answer.
