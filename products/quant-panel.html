ACTIVELY DEVELOPED

Built By Traders
Who Actually Use It

Quant Panel is developed alongside our own live trading, so it keeps evolving with what we actually need from it. Recent highlights from the v2.7 release:

Panel-wide zoom. A persistent on-screen zoom control (85–130%) scales the entire panel, not just the text, across local, RDP and VPS sessions.
Four-page PDF reports. Each algorithm's export now covers overview, monthly and trade detail, risk/return analysis, seasonality heatmap and methodology.
A full institutional metrics set. Realised CAGR, Sharpe, Sortino, annualised volatility, Calmar, Ulcer Index, historical VaR, expected shortfall, skew, kurtosis and return distribution, added to every report.
More honest drawdown reporting. Removed a floating-drawdown estimate that MT5's closed history can't actually reconstruct reliably, keeping underwater drawdown tied to each algo's real, realised equity peak.
Cleaner Manage mode. A dedicated status row and per-algo export button replaced the old Kill List workflow, so hiding a strategy no longer disturbs the table layout.

Full version history is available on request. Every update ships to existing owners at no extra cost.
