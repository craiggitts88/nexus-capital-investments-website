WHAT'S INCLUDED

The Full BPFX
Console

A browser-based lab that runs the complete strategy research methodology, from raw historical data to a finished, tested portfolio.

Foundation

Data Bridge

Eight years of M1 historical data, feeding directly into strategy generation so every candidate is built and tested on the same clean foundation.

Generation

Strategy Builder

Generates complete StrategyQuant project files with asset-specific block and range configurations, configurable genetic search budgets, and ATR-based stop and target calibration.

Assessment

Instrument Analyzer

Live market-difficulty assessment and real-cost analysis against ATR for any instrument, with entry-archetype identification and block-marking recommendations before you commit compute time to a run.

Combining

Portfolio Quality Plugin

A correlation matrix and 0-100 account scorecard built directly into StrategyQuant, auditing profit factor, drawdown and expectancy across a whole portfolio, not just individual strategies.

Validation

Sequential Out-of-Sample Testing

A 30% plus three-month out-of-sample reserve, applied automatically as part of the optimisation sequence, so a strategy has to prove itself on data it never saw during generation.

BPFX Trader Ledger

A private PAMM and funded-account tracking tool included with the Console, kept entirely local to your own account, for keeping a private record alongside your live results.
